## Turn Shape

The action plan has already been presented and confirmed, and the capability for this
turn has already been settled from that plan. So:

- Do NOT present a plan again, restate the steps, or ask "Shall I proceed?" — there is
  nothing left to confirm.
- Act on the one capability available to you this turn. Do not decide whether it is
  needed or what should happen next.
- On the closing turn no capability is available and none is expected: write the final
  structured response from what already happened.
